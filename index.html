§ 01

About

I'm an economics graduate and data analyst currently working as a REDI Economic Development Analyst in Colorado's San Luis Valley — one of the most economically complex rural regions in the American West. My work sits at the boundary of applied research and policy, where good data analysis can genuinely improve people's lives.

My academic background is unconventional in the best way: I competed as an NCAA Division II cross country and track athlete at Adams State University while earning a BS in Business Economics and an MBA in Finance, both Summa Cum Laude. Managing 20+ hours of training weekly alongside a 4.0 GPA taught me something no classroom can — sustained performance under pressure.

My long-term goal is to work as an economist and data analyst — bringing rigorous quantitative methods to the economic policy questions that matter most: regional inequality, labor market dynamics, rural development, and climate economics. I'm building that toolkit now through work in Excel, R, Tableau, SQL, and Stata, with SAS training underway.

When I'm not working with data, I'm writing about economics and policy on Substack, exploring the mountains outside Alamosa, and thinking about what it would take to make the San Luis Valley thrive economically for the next generation.

Data Projects

A growing collection of applied data analysis projects using R, Excel, and Tableau — focused on economic development, regional labor markets, and public policy. Projects link to GitHub repositories and live dashboards as they are completed.

R

San Luis Valley Labor Force Analysis

County-level labor force participation analysis using U.S. Census Bureau microdata and ACS datasets. Includes tidyverse data wrangling and ggplot2 visualizations of employment trends across the six-county district.

R

Regional Housing Cost Burden Study

Analysis of housing affordability across rural Colorado using HUD and ACS data. Regression modeling of cost burden against income levels, with geographic visualizations highlighting disparity across census tracts.

Coming Soon — In Development

R

Economic Inequality Trends: A Colorado Case Study

Time-series analysis of income inequality measures (Gini coefficient, income quintiles) across Colorado's rural and urban counties. Draws on academic work in climate and distributional economics.

Coming Soon — In Development

CEDS Budget Reconciliation Model

Multi-sheet grant budget model built for the Comprehensive Economic Development Strategy. Features Power Query data pulls, variance tracking, and compliance reporting dashboards used by state agency reviewers.

Coming Soon — In Development

Enterprise Zone Economic Indicator Dashboard

Annual reporting workbook synthesizing Enterprise Zone activity data, employment metrics, and tax credit usage into a clean, stakeholder-ready format for the Colorado Office of Economic Development.

Healthcare Revenue Pattern Analysis
